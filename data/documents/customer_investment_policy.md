# NorthGrid Transmission — Synthetic Investment Policy

> **Synthetic document created for demonstration only. It is not a document issued by any commercial vendor, network operator or regulator.**

## Portfolio principles

NorthGrid prioritises investments using a balanced view of safety and reliability, regulatory obligations, network capacity, customer outcomes, decarbonisation, deliverability and asset condition. A high project score is not sufficient on its own: the portfolio must remain within the approved capital envelope and mandatory regulatory commitments must be protected.

## Asset condition

Asset condition should be sourced from the current enterprise asset-management record where available. Missing, stale or conflicting condition records must be surfaced for human review rather than silently replaced. Where a condition value is unavailable, a neutral fallback may be used for exploratory scenario analysis, but the output must clearly flag that the recommendation relies on incomplete evidence.

## Regulatory commitments

Projects classified as mandatory due to licence, safety or formal regulatory commitments are treated as constrained investments. Scenario analysis may change the ranking of discretionary investments but must not automatically remove mandatory projects.

## Decision controls

Investment recommendations are decision support rather than autonomous approvals. Material changes in project ranking should be explainable in terms of source data, scoring assumptions, budget constraints and scenario weights. Human approval is required before a portfolio is adopted.
